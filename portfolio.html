Hardware Architecture Summary

Smart Blind Engineering Dossier

The Autonomous Smart Blind prototype tackles the critical balance between photosynthetic photon flux density (PPFD) and leaf-canopy temperature stress in controlled environment agriculture.

FIRMWARE TELEMETRY SPECIFICATION
- MCU: ESP32-WROOM-32D Dual Core 240MHz
- Telemetry Protocol: MQTT over TLS v1.3 (AWS IoT Core)
- Sensor: Adafruit BH1750 Ambient Light (I2C bus, 1-65535 lx)
- Actuation: 28BYJ-48 5V Stepper via ULN2003 Driver Array
- Polling Cycle: 300s deep-sleep interval during steady-state
