## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of computational rigor and modern agricultural intelligence. Built for a collegiate portfolio aimed at technical recruiters, enterprise engineering leads, and agricultural technology innovators, it balances youthful intellectual curiosity with poised, industry-ready professionalism.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Editorial Craft** with **Clean Functional Utility**. It rejects the generic startup software look in favor of an organic, grounded clarity:
- **Refined & Intellectual:** Editorial serif typography pairs with systematic, institutional sans-serif to convey academic depth and clear analytical thinking.
- **Rooted & Technical:** Color and tactile cues evoke environmental hardware, precision sensors, data telemetry, and sustainable yield systems.
- **Composed & High-Efficiency:** Generous breathing room, structural hairline rules, and unobtrusive micro-interactions make skimming coursework, technical GitHub projects, and agtech field research effortless for hiring teams.

## Colors

The color system is calibrated for an organic, high-contrast light mode that reflects field research and precision software.

- **Primary (`#1B3B2B` - Deep Forest Conifer):** Used for focal action anchors, key page markers, prominent badges, and dominant navigation highlights. Evokes deep canopy, precision field data, and natural vitality.
- **Secondary (`#2C4A5E` - Deep Slate Navy):** Represents systems architecture, databases, and network computing. Applied to secondary tags, metric highlights, and technical system annotations.
- **Tertiary (`#5F7A61` - Muted Sage Moss):** Connects hardware to field practice. Used for secondary badge fills, interactive state accents, and subtle graphical strokes.
- **Neutral Core (`#1C201E` - Charcoal Pine):** The bedrock text color. Off-black with a micro-drop of forest undertone ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against warm base planes.
- **Canvas & Surface System:**
  - **Base Background:** `#FBFBF9` (Alabaster Linen) — gentle on eyes, eliminating sterile blue monitor glare.
  - **Surface Layer 1 (Card/Container):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crisp White) — raises foreground content with sharp boundary definition.
  - **Surface Layer 2 (Muted Inset/Code block):** `#F3F4F0` (Field Moss Grey) — structural grounding for code, transcript blocks, and metadata tags.
- **Dividers & Strokes:** `#E2E5DF` (Soft Soil Border) — precise 1px definition providing rhythm without visual heaviness.

## Typography

The typography strategy leverages **Newsreader** for headings to deliver an organic, scholarly, and articulate atmosphere akin to modern technical journals, paired with **Public Sans** for functional, clean, and highly legible body text and interface markers.

- **Headlines:** Set in Newsreader with subtle optical negative tracking. Its organic serifs frame project titles, academic research statements, and personal narratives with warmth and maturity.
- **Body & Data Displays:** Set in Public Sans. Engineered originally for government and public-sector accessibility, it produces clean vertical rhythm, wide apertures, and uncompromising readability across mobile and desktop displays.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Set in medium and bold weights of Public Sans with expanded tracking on uppercase indicators (`label-caps`) to cleanly annotate technologies, GPA stats, status badges, and sensor metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an orderly **12-column responsive fluid grid** capped at a maximum width of `72rem` (1152px), creating a focused editorial reading line.

- **Vertical Cadence:** Based on an 8px architectural grid unit (`0.5rem`). Section margins expand generously (`space-3xl` and `space-4xl`) to provide recruiters an unhurried, focused evaluation experience.
- **Breakpoint Adjustments:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, 32px gutters (`space-xl`), 64px page margins. Case studies utilize asymmetric layouts (e.g., 4-column sticky metadata sidebar paired with an 8-column narrative body).
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters (`space-lg`), 32px page margins. Multi-column cards collapse to dual-column stacks.
  - **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters (`space-md`), 16px page margins. Horizontal scrolling is enabled for tag chips and project image carousels; all key columns stack vertically into a single unified flow.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **tonal surface stacking** combined with **fine hairline framing** and **diffused botanic-tinted shadows**. Heavy drop shadows are strictly avoided to maintain crisp professionalism.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FBFBF9` flat linen backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px continuous hairline outline in `#E2E5DF` and a very subtle ambient drop shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(28, 32, 30, 0.04), 0 6px 16px rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.03);`
- **Level 2 (Hovered Project Surfaces & Navigation Bar):** Slightly elevated card state during pointer hover, and frosted sticky top header:
  - Header: `background: rgba(251, 251, 249, 0.88); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border-bottom: 1px solid #E2E5DF;`
  - Hover Elevation: `transform: translateY(-2px); box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(28, 32, 30, 0.06), 0 12px 24px rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.05); border-color: #CBD0C7;`
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Flyout Menus):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px rgba(28, 32, 30, 0.08), 0 20px 48px rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.06); border: 1px solid #E2E5DF;`

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Level 1 (Soft)** roundedness to balance modern software precision with approachable tactile warmth.

- **Interactive Core:** Buttons, text input fields, and tags carry a controlled `0.375rem` (6px) radius, preventing the childlike softness of full pill buttons while avoiding the austere severity of razor-sharp rectangles.
- **Containers & Artifact Cards:** Project showcases, academic coursework grids, and repository displays utilize an `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- **Status Pills & Metric Pills:** Retain micro-proportional curves (`4px` to `6px`) to present concise data points (e.g., "Python", "IoT LoRaWAN", "Dean's List") as structured computational modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "View GitHub", "Download Resume"):**
  - Solid Deep Forest (`#1B3B2B`) fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` Public Sans semi-bold text.
  - Height: 44px (touch accessible). Horizontal padding: 20px. Border radius: 6px.
  - Hover: Background shifts to `#244B38` with a 1px upward transition. Active: Shifts to `#142C20`.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Read Field Report", "Contact"):**
  - Transparent background with a crisp 1px border in `#CBD0C7` and Slate Navy text (`#2C4A5E`).
  - Hover: Background fills with `#F3F4F0` and border shifts to `#5F7A61`.
- **Text Action:**
  - Newsreader italic or Public Sans semi-bold with a persistent 1px green underline offset by 4px.

### Badges & Technical Chips
- **Domain Categories (AgTech vs. CIS):**
  - **AgTech:** `#EDF3EE` background, `#1B3B2B` border-accent, `#1B3B2B` text, 0.75rem uppercase bold.
  - **CIS / Software:** `#EBF1F5` background, `#2C4A5E` border-accent, `#2C4A5E` text.
- Shape: 4px border radius, 4px vertical padding, 10px horizontal padding.

### Project & Research Cards
- Pure white container surfaced with a 1px hairline border in `#E2E5DF`.
- Inset structure: Top area hosts crisp image or live schematic preview framed with an internal 1px border. Bottom area holds category badge, Newsreader headline, summary body copy, and a technical stack pill cluster.
- Smooth transition on hover (`transform: translateY(-2px)` and border tint to `#5F7A61`).

### Lists & Timelines (Coursework & Leadership)
- Clean, vertical ruled line (`1px solid #E2E5DF`) connecting semesters and milestones.
- Milestone nodes: 8px solid forest green circular dots aligned with dates in bold Public Sans, accompanied by role titles in Newsreader.

### Form Inputs & Inquiries
- Base state: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with 1px border `#CBD0C7`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#1B3B2B` with an accessible, dual-ring glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.12)`.
- Labels float top-left in Public Sans `label-md` with neutral charcoal coloring.

### Code & Sensor Metric Display (Specialty Portfolio Component)
- Background `#F3F4F0` container with a fine `#E2E5DF` edge border.
- Monospace snippets rendered for embedded sensor payloads, SQL schemas, or IoT network packets, paired with copy-to-clipboard micro-buttons.
